# The panel

Click the **Send to AI** icon in the Activity Bar (left edge of VS Code).

- The file tree is pre-ticked with everything that is not ignored.
- `.gitignore`, `.sendtoaiignore`, lock files, binaries and secrets (`.env*`, keys, credential files) are always left out.
- Token estimates update as you tick and untick.

Nothing leaves your machine. The bundle goes to your clipboard and nowhere else.
