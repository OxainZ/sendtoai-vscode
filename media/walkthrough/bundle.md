# Bundle to Clipboard

1. Type what you want the AI to do in the **prompt** box (optional, but it drives the relevance ranking).
2. Click **Bundle to Clipboard**.
3. Paste into ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or any chat.

**Formats**: Standard, Claude XML, Compact (comments stripped), Minimal.

**Modes**: whole project, open tabs, or git changes (Pro).

The free tier keeps the 50 most relevant files for your prompt. Pro ($9.99 once, 30-day refund, 5 machines) bundles the whole project and can fit it to your AI's context window.
